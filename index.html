Our Heritage

Born from a Passion for Pakistan's Coffee Renaissance

In an era of instant blends and rushed routines, Coffee Wala was founded in the cultural heart of Pakistan with one uncompromising devotion: elevating specialty coffee craft while honoring our rich tradition of heartfelt baithaks and hospitality.

From the aroma-filled cafes of Lahore and Karachi to cold-drip brews inspired by the high altitudes of Gilgit-Baltistan and the Karakoram, we unite world-class specialty roasting with the soul of Pakistani gatherings.

Bean to Cup

The Four Rituals of Our Craft

Scroll down to journey through our meticulous brewing philosophy.

STEP 01

Curated Global Micro-Lots

Directly sourcing ethical high-altitude Arabica green beans from renowned specialty coffee cooperatives worldwide.

STEP 02

Artisanal Drum Roasting in Lahore

Calibrating convection airflow and roast profiles in our drum to unlock sweet caramel, toasted spices, and rich cocoa butter.

STEP 03

The 28-Second Pull

Dialed-in 9 bars of steam pressure delivering golden tiger-striped crema with nuanced aromatic complexity.

STEP 04

Slow Sensory Immersion

Whether a 20-hour steeped nitro cold brew with mountain saffron or a velvety flat white, each cup is an invitation to pause and savor.
